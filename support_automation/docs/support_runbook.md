# Support Runbook

## Triage
1. Confirm service, severity, issue type, and customer impact.
2. Classify issue family.
3. Review recommended runbook action.
4. Route to probable owner.

## Troubleshooting
- Check recent deployments.
- Check dependency health.
- Check logs and latency metrics.
- Confirm recurrence pattern.
- Validate customer/business impact.

## Resolution
Resolve once the owner confirms fix, workaround, or rollback.
