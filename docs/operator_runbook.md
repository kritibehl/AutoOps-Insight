# AutoOps Operator Runbook

## When an incident is new
1. Review issue family and confidence.
2. Check recurrence and customer blocker status.
3. Move state from `new` to `triaged`.

## When escalation is required
1. Verify issue family and action.
2. Attach PM summary and engineering bug report.
3. Move state to `escalated`.

## When suppressing
Suppress only if duplicate, noisy, or already covered by a known incident.

## When resolving
Resolve after engineering action or support workaround is confirmed.

## Reopening
Reopen when recurrence appears after resolution.
