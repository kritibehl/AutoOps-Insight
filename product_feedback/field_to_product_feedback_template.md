# Field-to-Product Feedback Template

## Field symptom

Describe the recurring customer or partner issue.

## Affected workflow

List affected workflow, service, or integration path.

## Evidence reviewed

- incident IDs
- logs or telemetry
- API response
- SQL/reporting evidence
- customer blocker summary

## Frequency

How often this pattern appears.

## Customer friction

What user/customer/partner action is blocked?

## Recommended product change

What should engineering or product improve?

## Suggested validation

What test, evaluation, or dashboard metric should confirm the improvement?

## Owner

Suggested service owner, product owner, or support owner.
