# Escalation Deadlines

## SLA policy

| Severity | Triage target | Resolution target | Escalation required |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| critical | 15 minutes | 4 hours | yes |
| high | 30 minutes | 8 hours | yes |
| medium | 60 minutes | 24 hours | conditional |
| low | 240 minutes | 72 hours | no |

## Escalation rules

1. Critical incidents route immediately to service owner and incident commander.
2. High-severity incidents require engineering escalation if not triaged within 30 minutes.
3. Medium-severity incidents remain with support unless recurrence or customer impact increases.
4. SLA-breached incidents must include owner, next action, and business impact summary.

## Operational review

SLA breaches are reviewed in the weekly business review and service-owner dashboard.
